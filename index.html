Why Bergknapp?

BÆRG-knahp, with the k pronounced.
Or say it however you like.

Bergknapp is Norwegian for stonecrop: the succulent that grows on bare rock and turf roofs. Literally “rock button.” It gets by on almost nothing, and it’s the plant on the logo.

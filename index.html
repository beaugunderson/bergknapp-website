Bergknapp Softworks GitHub

Small, fast software for the Mac.

Each of these started as something that bothered me on my own Mac. They are native, offline, and quick, and they leave your files exactly where they were.

Knap

knap.rocks

Find the note before you finish typing. Instant search and editing over a folder of Markdown you already have, beside an Obsidian vault or on its own.

Mac App Store · $9.99 once

Spacebar

spacebar.tools

Press Space, see inside any file. Quick Look for zip files and everything in them, GPS rides, databases, data tables, email archives, and more.

macOS Quick Look extensions

Tuck

tuck.bar

A little less menu bar. A tiny menu bar manager with about 0% idle CPU and no always-on event tap.

Free forever · GPLv3

About the name. Bergknapp is Norwegian for stonecrop, the sedum that grows on bare rock and turf roofs: literally "rock button". It gets by on almost nothing, and it is the plant on the logo. Say it however you like; in Norway it is BÆRG-knahp, with the k pronounced.
